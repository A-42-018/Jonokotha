# Problem statement (DRAFT)

**Project:** Jonokotha (জনকথা) — AIBC 2026, Competition 1, CivicSphere AI.

## Problem
Citizens in Bangladesh have no simple, trackable way to tell their elected
representative about neighbourhood problems (roads, drains, water, power,
waste). Reports travel by phone calls, personal contacts and social media; they
get lost, duplicated, or reach the wrong agency, and the citizen never learns
what happened. MP offices have no structured view of what their area needs.

## Who it affects
- Citizens, especially those with low bandwidth, limited literacy or no
  English: they need to report in plain spoken Bangla from a phone.
- MP offices and staff: they need a triaged queue, not a flood of messages.
- Service agencies: they receive vague, misrouted complaints.

## Our approach
A Bangla-first, mobile-first installable web app (PWA). A citizen types or
speaks a problem; an AI Intake Agent drafts the title, category and area; the
citizen edits and submits. The office sees an AI summary, similar cases and a
cited agency suggestion. **Staff approve every outward action**; the citizen
sees a public status timeline with a reference ID.

## Scope of the preliminary slice
Sign-in, constituency selection, report (type/speak), reference ID, public
issue page, office queue with AI panel, staff approval, bn/en interface, and a
clearly labelled demo constituency with agencies linked to official sources.
