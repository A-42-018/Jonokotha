# Terms of use and takedown process (draft)

Draft for counsel review; not yet reviewed. Plain language first; the team fills [brackets].

1. **What this is.** A civic reporting and communication tool. It is not an emergency service, not legal advice, and not an official government channel unless an office says so on its page. In danger, call 999.
2. **Your account.** One person, one account, accurate information. You are responsible for what you post.
3. **What you may not post.** Abuse, threats, hate, false claims presented as fact, other people's private data (phone, address, ID), spam, anything unlawful. Photos should not show faces or private property unless necessary.
4. **Public content.** Reports, ideas and questions you mark or that are published may be seen by anyone. Offices may reply publicly; replies are approved by a person.
5. **AI.** AI drafts text for you and for offices. Drafts can be wrong. You and staff decide what is sent.
6. **No promises.** Offices are not obliged to act or reply within a set time. Responsiveness figures are descriptive, not rankings.
7. **Moderation and takedown.** Moderators can hide content that breaks these terms, with a written reason shown in the audit log. If your content is hidden you can write to [contact]; a different moderator reviews within [5 working days]. Legal takedown requests: write to [contact] with what, where and on what basis; we act promptly and record the request (`docs/aibc/runbooks/content-takedown.md`).
8. **Suspension.** Accounts can be suspended for serious or repeated breaches; the reason is recorded.
9. **Your data.** See the privacy notice. You can export and delete your data.
10. **Changes and law.** [Governing law, operator, changes procedure; counsel to complete].
