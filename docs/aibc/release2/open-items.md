# Release 2 (backend Phase 8): what is built and what still needs people, accounts, an official API, a budget or legal review

Date: 2026-11-17. Branch `claude/backend-phase8`, migrations 0080–0083, local stack only; nothing is applied to the hosted project. Source table: `docs/backend.md` Phase 8. API: `docs/api-v1.md` "Phase 8".

Rule used throughout: a feature that depends on someone else's system, money or legal position is built only as far as code, schema and documents honestly allow. The platform never shows an "officially registered", "binding" or "verified" state that no one has recorded, files nothing on a citizen's behalf, and publishes no score for an MP.

## Needs a person, an account, an official API, a budget or a legal review
| Item | Why it is open | Who / what unblocks it |
|---|---|---|
| B8.2 Agency integrations | No Bangladeshi agency has been found to publish an API or an authorised workflow for receiving referrals, and none was contacted. Built: the integration record (B5.4), an Ed25519 **public key** per confirmed integration, and a signed-receipt endpoint that registers a referral only with a valid signature from that agency. It is tested with generated keys only. **No key is seeded and nothing is registered by this code today.** The flag `official_registration` is off | Team lead: an agency partner that agrees on a receipt format (or a different proof); an administrator then confirms the integration and records its public key. A real agency may need a different proof than our signed text, which is a small change |
| B8.3 Volunteers and kiosks | Needs people: who recruits and trains volunteers, who is accountable if one misuses the role, and what a kiosk is physically. The conduct rules and the consent text are **drafts** (`reviewed: false`) and the consent sheet for paper and kiosk use does not exist. The citizen has no account, so no one but the volunteer receives updates; a "claim this case with my own account" step was not built | Counsel and native-Bangla review of both texts (`docs/aibc/pilot/counsel-review.md`); a pilot office; then switch `assisted_reporting` on |
| B8.4 Open-data licence | `CC-BY-4.0` is a **placeholder**: choosing a licence for data about public reports is a legal and policy decision. The catalogue says `reviewed: false`. A cell under 5 is dropped, but with several slices of one dataset a determined reader can still narrow small counts by subtraction; no formal privacy analysis (for example a differential-privacy budget) was done | Counsel: licence. Privacy review before wide use. Then `open_data` on |
| B8.6 Participatory budgeting | An office must really control a budget **and** agree to follow the result. The platform cannot know either: it records a verified budget document, a public pledge, the MP's approval and the recorded outcome, and says so. The pledge is not a legal commitment. No pilot office exists, so no cycle has run outside tests | A pilot office with a real budget line, counsel on the pledge wording. Flag `participatory_budgeting` off |
| B8.7 RTI helper | Deadlines (20 and 30 working days, 30 days to appeal) and the Information Commission link are a starting draft from public descriptions of the RTI Act 2009 and are **not confirmed** (`app_settings.rti_rules.reviewed = false`; channels `verified: false`). Public holidays are unknown so a due date is the earliest possible. The Bangla and English drafts are plain templates, not reviewed by a lawyer or a native reader. The helper never files; no integration with an official portal exists | Counsel confirms the rules and wording; native review; update `rti_rules`; then `rti_helper` on |
| B8.8 MP records | There is no parliamentary data feed here: records are entered by hand from official sources (moderators or administrators, second person publishes). The official source's terms of reuse were not checked. The AI summary is a draft with number grounding and a word-list check for evaluative words in Bangla and English; the list is a heuristic and has not been tested on real records, and no native-reader review happened (no model key in tests) | An editorial team and a decision on which official sources to use; counsel on reuse; native review of drafts. Flag `mp_records` off |
| B8.9 Open311 | Read side only. Posting is refused because a report needs a verified account and (for assisted cases) a consent record, which GeoReport's anonymous model cannot carry. No third-party Open311 client was tested against it; JSON and XML are covered by our own tests. The flag `open311_api` is off: exposing a public API is the operator's decision | Operator: switch on; optional later: authenticated POST bridge |
| B8.10 Second country | Built: readiness gates and the activation block. **No second country was chosen and no second-country data was written**: a country adapter needs its own legal, electoral and data-protection review (`docs/aibc/release2/second-country-pilot.md`). Nothing in the code can decide that a review was done: it records the document reference and the named reviewer | Counsel and a local partner in the chosen country |
| Frontend | Screens, labels and the volunteer, RTI, open-data and MP-record pages are the frontend lane (`docs/api-v1.md`, Phase 8 handoff) | Frontend session |
| Migrations on the hosted project | 0021–0023, 0030–0036, 0060–0062, 0070–0074 and 0080–0083 are not applied there (`docs/aibc/runbooks/deploy-and-rollback.md`) | The user, through the approval-gated migrate workflow |
